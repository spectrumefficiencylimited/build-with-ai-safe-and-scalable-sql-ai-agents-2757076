Resolving ambiguity with deterministic context
In the previous lesson, we created a simple agent by simply wrapping the chain and its supporting arguments. And it works like magic, generating the right SQL query and returning the correct data. But there is a question hiding beneath the surface. How does the model know what the data actually means? In this lesson, we'll look beyond what the LLM generates and focus on why it makes certain decisions and what happens when it doesn't have enough information. You will see how ambiguity creeps in, why models sometimes guess incorrectly, and how we can guide them by injecting the right kind of context at the right time. Here is where things get interesting. Giving the question, the LLM selects the terms with the highest likelihood of answering it. This probabilistic reasoning is what makes LLM so powerful.
